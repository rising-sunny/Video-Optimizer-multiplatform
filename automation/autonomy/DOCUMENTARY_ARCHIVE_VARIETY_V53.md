# Skylab documentary: evidence and visual variety v53

The creator requested a new ten-minute English horizontal documentary for Stunning
Revelations, using vidIQ and incorporating the Chrome Dome audit. The selected
original framing follows the TWO repairs that made Skylab habitable and usable:
thermal protection and electrical power. It avoids the later unflown rescue craft.

vidIQ research returned 61,925 estimated monthly searches for the broad `skylab`
term (3,102 in the US), and 3,817 for `skylab space station`. These are estimates,
not English-audience views or predictions. Recent small-channel examples are not
reliable breakout evidence. Both outlier calls failed; private per-video analytics
were unavailable. The owned six-account metadata snapshot was fully paginated;
Apollo 12 and Apollo 13 were already covered on Facts. No Skylab title was found.
vidIQ has only three credits left; title scoring and thumbnail generation there
are unavailable. No credit purchase or provider fallback is performed by this code.

The native model prompt previously requested only 8-10 sources. It now encourages
16-24 when actually offered, without weakening type, attribution or rights checks.
For the explicit Skylab topic, official NASA factual snapshots and a bounded
multi-archive catalogue are prepared BEFORE planning. Moving files are discovered
first, then varied photos of launch, damage, engineering, training and repair.

The selected plan must show 18 distinct resources, including at least two video
sources, and reference every selected asset. This is not a claim that every source
is available, downloaded, relevant or reviewed: catalogue, download, timeline and
actual final MP4 receipts remain authoritative. Missing evidence blocks rather
than silently recreating Chrome Dome's small repetitive pool.

Official Commons page IDs may shorten credit links. Canonical links, complete
provider records, creators and rights stay in the receipts. The IDs are never
guessed. All original model budgets and review limits remain; no publication,
timer change or original-job mutation is included. Prepared factual text avoids
spending model tool calls to re-fetch evidence already pinned by the controller.

Native image decode, clip movement/duration, narration timing, SRT, music rights,
technical QA and actual visual assessment must pass. A deployment is not a ready
documentary. The thumbnail is a separate deliverable and not implicitly approved
by the native video's `video_only` quality scope.
