TITLE
Skylab: NASA's Space Station Broke Before Its Crew Arrived

DESCRIPTION — preserve all source and music credits
In May 1973, Skylab reached orbit damaged and overheating before its first crew arrived. Follow the ground teams and Conrad, Weitz and Kerwin through two repairs: a parasol to cool the workshop and a spacewalk to release its solar wing. NASA photographs, ground tests, illustrations and mission film are identified by context. No exterior film of the June 7 repair is claimed.
