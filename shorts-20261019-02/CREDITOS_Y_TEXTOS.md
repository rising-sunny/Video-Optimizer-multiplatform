## Desmond Doss: He Went to War Without a Gun

Canal: stunning-facts-of-history. Duración: 53.4 s. Clips reales: 10 s.

Desmond Doss served as a U.S. Army medic without carrying a weapon. On Okinawa in 1945, he stayed with wounded men under fire and lowered them by rope to safety. His Medal of Honor citation describes repeated rescues; he received the award from President Harry Truman on 12 October 1945. His story inspired Hacksaw Ridge.

No movie footage or filmed rescue is used. The moving scenes show the Okinawa Memorial Day ceremony in Itoman on 23 June 2025, clearly labeled as later context, not Doss or his unit in 1945.

## Starfish Prime: The U.S. Nuclear Test in Space

Canal: stunning-revelations. Duración: 53.4 s. Clips reales: 14 s.

On 9 July 1962 UTC (8 July in Hawaii), the United States detonated Starfish Prime about 400 km above the Pacific. The yield was about 1.4 megatons. Its electromagnetic pulse caused electrical damage in Hawaii, roughly 1,450 km away, including about 300 streetlights. Artificial radiation belts also damaged satellites. This historical test does not establish claims about any current secret weapon or UFO.

Photos show Starfish Prime and its glow as seen from Honolulu. Moving scenes are real footage from the official 1962 High Altitude Weapons Effects film, covering several Operation Fishbowl tests; launch, instrumentation and program operations are labeled as program context, not claimed to show the Starfish detonation itself. Original audio removed.
