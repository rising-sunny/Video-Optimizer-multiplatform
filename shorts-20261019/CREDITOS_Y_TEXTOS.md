## Nicholas Winton: 669 Children and a Way Out
Canal: stunning-facts-of-history. Duración: 54.4 s. Video real: 13.5 s.

In 1939, Nicholas Winton helped a wider rescue network bring 669 children, mostly Jewish, from Czechoslovakia to safety in Britain. He worked on sponsors, paperwork and homes in Britain; Doreen Warriner, Trevor Chadwick and many others played vital roles. Survivors reunited with him on BBC television in 1988.

Archival video: a separate Prague child evacuation on 11 January 1939 (the Hansi Neumann flight), used as period context, not footage of all 669 children or the BBC reunion. Source: USHMM via Wikimedia Commons; public domain as reviewed there. The Harwich photo shows children from Vienna in December 1938, also context.

## Acoustic Kitty: The CIA Tried to Turn a Cat into a Spy
Canal: stunning-revelations. Duración: 53.9 s. Video real: 17 s.

In the 1960s, the CIA explored using a cat fitted with a microphone and radio transmitter for eavesdropping. A 1967 assessment concluded the technique was not practical for intelligence work in real foreign conditions.

The famous claim that a taxi killed the cat was disputed by a former CIA technical-services director. Its fate should not be presented as settled fact. No successful operational spy mission is established here, and the often-repeated cost figure is omitted.

All cats shown are modern pets, NOT CIA test animals. No surgery or original operational footage is shown. The later headquarters photo is context.
