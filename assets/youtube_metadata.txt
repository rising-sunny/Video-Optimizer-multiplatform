TITLE
Soviet Submarine B-59: The Nuclear Torpedo That Almost Started WW3

DESCRIPTION
October 27, 1962. In the Sargasso Sea, a Soviet submarine is trapped beneath a ring of American warships dropping explosives to force it up. Inside, the air is running out — and in its torpedo room is a nuclear torpedo the Americans know nothing about.

This is the story of submarine B-59 and Vasily Arkhipov, told with real archival footage, the original deck log of the destroyer USS Beale, the White House ExComm transcript and President Kennedy's own words from October 22, 1962.

What we know — and what we don't: the nuclear torpedo, the practice depth charges and the surfacing of B-59 are documented. The scene inside the submarine comes from the recollections of crew member Vadim Orlov and other participants; his account of the order to assemble the torpedo is disputed, and the Russian documents released in 2022 do not mention the incident.
